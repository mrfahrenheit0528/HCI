Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about the ORUS platform.

ORUS or the Online Registration and Update System is a web-based system that provides an end-to-end process for registration of taxpayers and updating of their registration information. It allows seamless, paperless transactions with the BIR directly from your browser.

Any individual or non-individual taxpayer who wishes to register with the BIR or update their existing registration information can use ORUS. This includes professionals, sole proprietors, corporations, partnerships, and other juridical entities.

  • New Business Registration (Head Office and Branches)
  • Registration of Facilities
  • Updates of Registration Information
  • Cancellation of TIN / Business Classification
  • Verification of TIN

You can create an account by clicking the "Log In / Register" button on the portal homepage. You will need a valid email address and your existing TIN (if already registered). For new taxpayers without a TIN, you can directly proceed to New Registration which will provisionally create an account profile linked to your application.

If the system cannot validate your TIN during account creation, you may need to visit your Revenue District Office (RDO) to verify your registration status or resolve any data discrepancies in the BIR database. You may also contact the BIR Customer Assistance Division.
