Bureau of Internal Revenue

Bringing in Revenues for Nation-building

Online Registration and Update System (ORUS) is a web-based system that provides an end-to-end process for registration of taxpayers and updating of their registration information. It is designed to streamline transactions with the Bureau, making compliance easier, faster, and more accessible from anywhere.

Mandate

The Bureau shall comprehend the assessment and collection of all national internal revenue taxes, fees, and charges, and the enforcement of all forfeitures, penalties, and fines connected therewith. (Section 2 of the National Internal Revenue Code of 1997).

Mission

We collect taxes through just enforcement of tax laws for nation-building and the upliftment of the lives of Filipinos.

Guiding Principle

Service Excellence with Integrity and Professionalism.
